Why 1,000 chapatis?

On Sunday 20th September, Chef Abala and the team are cooking and serving 1,000 chapatis to street kids around Ruai — a single afternoon built entirely on community generosity. It's simple: gather ingredients, cook together, and put a warm meal and a smile in front of a child who needs one.

We plead for your kind support. Every chapati values — and every chapati is funded by someone like you.

Where KES 20 goes

We worked out the numbers so nothing is a guess. A working budget of KES 20,000 covers everything needed to cook and serve 1,000 chapatis — which comes to roughly 20 shillings per chapati.

  • FlourCost of flour for 1,000 chapatis
  • Cooking oilOil for frying
  • Cooking equipmentPans, jikos, utensils
  • FuelGas / charcoal
  • Packaging & distributionWrapping and serving
